## Density stress probe (validator-added)

The regime that produced 15% FMR in b25c990 — same merchant, identical amounts, transactions minutes apart — is no longer present at scale in the gate dataset (see Decision 1), so I tested the engine against it directly: 10/50/200 identical Screwfix transactions at 3-minute spacing with matching receipts.

**Result: zero false matches at every density. The engine routes the entire population to `conflict`.** Recall collapses to zero in this regime, which is exactly the trade ADR-001 commits to: these pairs are genuinely indistinguishable, and "ambiguity is surfaced, not guessed away." The precision guarantee — the one that protects tax records — holds in the worst case the synthetic gate no longer exercises.

## Two decisions requiring ratification before "Phase 0 complete"

**Decision 1 — Generator composition was changed after seeing failures.** The implementer's own remediation notes state it: *"prime-hour txn spacing (37h) to break 48h settlement aliasing."* Generated transactions are now spaced 37 hours apart, so temporal density never grows with n; the dense/ambiguous populations are fixed clusters of 4–8 records regardless of scale. Consequences: metrics are bit-identical across seeds; the headline FMR=0 at n=10⁴ is measured predominantly on well-separated pairs; the hard-class recall floor is computed on samples of 2–4. This is transparent (documented in the repo) and arguably *more* realistic — a sole trader's year is not 10⁴ transactions in one week — and the density probe above shows the engine is safe in the regime the gate stopped measuring. But dataset composition determines what the thresholds mean, and changing it after a failed run is a protocol matter regardless of merit.

*Recommendation:* ratify the spacing with written rationale, **and** require the ambiguous/near-duplicate populations to scale proportionally (e.g. a configurable 5–10% of n) with their own per-class assertions, plus adopt the density-stress probe as a permanent scenario class. Then re-run and re-commit the gate.

**Decision 2 — `candidatesDistinguishable` retained.** Within-ambiguity-margin pairs that differ on a primary signal (amount or merchant by >0.01, or temporal delta by >120 s) resolve to the better candidate instead of conflicting. This is the B4 narrowing, renamed, documented — and the repo's own checklist (TESTING.md: "No `signalIndistinguishable`-style narrowing unless accepted via ADR amendment") acknowledges it needs ratification it doesn't yet have. On the merits it is defensible: two same-merchant candidates two minutes apart *are* distinguishable evidence-wise, and the density probe confirms the rule fails safe (conflicts, not false matches, when deltas compress). But it changes D2's resolution semantics.

*Recommendation:* ratify as an ADR-001 amendment with the three thresholds (0.01 / 0.01 / 120 s) moved into `Config` rather than constants — they are tuning parameters and belong under config-as-data discipline.

Also ratify retroactively: config tuning (MinConfidence 0.75→0.82, AmbiguityMargin 0.03→0.02, new `MinMerchantForCandidate` 0.55) — documented in the remediation table, which serves as a coarse tuning log; future changes should get per-change entries.

2. **Doc drift:** ADR-001's generator-composition amendment says ambiguous **4%**; code and ratification response say **2%** (the 2% figure is the reasoned one — conflict entries double-count against the 5% ceiling). The ADR is the authority document; correct it to 2%. Also a stale comment in `generator.go` ("~7.5% each").
3. **Known, accepted property worth restating:** gate metrics remain bit-identical across seeds — population sizes are deterministic fractions of n and the noise jitter never crosses decision boundaries. This was a documented consequence of ratified Decision 1; it means the synthetic gate's variance is ~zero and the first real variance arrives with Phase 2 data. No action; just don't read the five-seed agreement as statistical robustness.
